Modern solar films are engineered to be nearly invisible from the inside. You'll notice less glare and heat, but your outward view remains clear and undistorted.

Solar films primarily block heat, UV, and glare. Security films are thicker and hold shattered glass together upon impact — protecting against break-ins, accidents, and explosions. Some films combine both properties.
